## H4-01 — AI Can Accelerate Blueprint Authoring

> **Hypothesis:** AI can substantially increase the rate at which human SMEs can create, extend and maintain production blueprints.

### Summary

AI can assist SMEs in translating architectural, domain and implementation knowledge into the structured artifacts as blueprints.

### Implementation

SME Expertise + AI → Blueprint → Validation → Certified Production Knowledge.

### Value — Today

AI can assist with portions of blueprint creation and maintenance.

### Value — Tomorrow

AI increasingly reduces the human effort required to author and maintain the expanding blueprint continuum.

### Evidence

[AI Blueprint Authoring](../../evidence/ai); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md)

### Vision

The rate of production-knowledge creation increasingly exceeds the rate at which human SMEs could author it alone.
