## H4-03 — AI Can Improve Blueprint Quality

> **Hypothesis:** Generation, verification and operational results can provide feedback that improves the quality of the underlying blueprint.

### Summary

Create structured artifacts and verification results that can provide AI with evidence about how effectively a blueprint produces systems.

### Implementation

Blueprint → Generated System → Verification & Results → AI Analysis → Blueprint Improvement.

### Value — Today

Blueprints can be tested and validated against defined requirements.

### Value — Tomorrow

Production evidence increasingly informs continuous blueprint improvement.

### Evidence

[Verification](../../evidence/verification/README.md); [AI](../../evidence/ai); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md)

### Vision

Blueprints become increasingly capable through evidence gathered from their own use.
