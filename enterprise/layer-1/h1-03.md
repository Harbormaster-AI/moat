## H1-03 — Production Can Be Separated From Individual Developers

> **Hypothesis:** A significant portion of software-production knowledge can be captured independently of the individual developer who originally possessed or applied it.

### Summary

Capture production knowledge in blueprints, models, configuration and policies so that defined capabilities can be reproduced without recreating the original implementation process.

### Implementation

Expert Knowledge → Blueprint + Model + Policies → Repeatable Production Process → Generated System.

### Value — Today

Teams can reuse established architectural and implementation patterns without depending entirely on the original expert.

### Value — Tomorrow

Enterprise production capability becomes increasingly independent of individual employee availability, turnover and organizational boundaries.
