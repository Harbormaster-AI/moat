## H2-04 — Industry and Business-Domain Knowledge Can Become Reusable Production IP

> **Hypothesis:** Knowledge about business domains and industry processes can be represented as reusable domain models that contribute directly to software production.

### Summary

Domain models capture entities, relationships and business concepts that can be reused when producing systems within a business or industry domain.

### Implementation

Domain Knowledge → Domain Model → Blueprint + Configuration → Generated System.

### Value — Today

Teams can begin from established representations of business-domain capabilities rather than modeling common concepts repeatedly.

### Value — Tomorrow

Enterprise domain knowledge can become reusable production IP shared across applications and business units.

### Evidence

[Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models/README.md); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md)

### Vision

The enterprise's understanding of its business becomes part of the executable foundation from which future systems are produced.
