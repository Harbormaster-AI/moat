## H2-02 — Technology Expertise Can Become Technology Blueprints

> **Hypothesis:** Expertise in a technology can be encoded as an executable blueprint rather than remaining primarily a collection of certifications, experience and individual knowledge.

### Summary

Blueprints can represent both generic technology capabilities and targeted technology solutions.

### Implementation

Spring Boot blueprint provides a Restful foundation; Axon Framework 4 blueprint adds an event-sourcing solution; Corda blueprint then adds distributed-ledger capabilities.

### Value — Today

Technology expertise can be represented in reusable blueprints.

### Value — Tomorrow

A growing portfolio of technology and solution blueprints can cover a wide range of strategic technologies.

### Evidence

[Spring Boot Blueprint](../../evidence/blueprints/solutions/spring-boot); [Axon 4 Blueprint](../../evidence/blueprints/solutions/axon4); [Corda Blueprint](../../evidence/blueprints/solutions/corda); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md)

### Vision

Technology partnerships become opportunities to build executable solution capabilities around those technologies.
