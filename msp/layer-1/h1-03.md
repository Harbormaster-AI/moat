## H1-03 — Production Can Be Separated From Individual Developers

> **Hypothesis:** Significant portions of system production can be separated from individual developer knowledge by encoding production knowledge in reusable blueprints, models and policies.

### Summary

Capture production knowledge outside the individual developer and applies that knowledge consistently across generated systems.

### Implementation

Production Knowledge → Blueprint + Model + Policies → Repeatable Generation → Consistent Systems.

### Value — Today

Teams can reuse established architectures, technology patterns and production rules rather than recreating them independently.

### Value — Tomorrow

A greater portion of system production can be performed from organizational knowledge rather than depending on specific individuals.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models/README.md); [Production Rules](../../evidence/production-rules)

### Vision

Software-production capability becomes an organizational asset that persists beyond individual developers and engagements.
