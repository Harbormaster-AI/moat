## H2-02 — Technology Expertise Can Become Technology Blueprints

> **Hypothesis:** Expertise in application, infrastructure and platform technologies can be encoded into reusable technology blueprints.

### Summary

Encode technology-specific production knowledge into blueprints that define how a technology is configured, integrated and deployed as part of a generated system.

### Implementation

Technology Expertise → Technology Blueprint → Generated Configuration → Deployable System.

### Value — Today

Technology patterns can be packaged into reusable production capabilities.

### Value — Tomorrow

A growing library of technology blueprints can reduce the effort required to implement and operate supported technology patterns across customers.

### Evidence

[Technology Blueprints](../../evidence/technology-blueprints); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md)

### Vision

The MSP's technology expertise becomes a continuously reusable production library rather than knowledge recreated for each customer.
