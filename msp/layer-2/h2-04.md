## H2-04 — Industry Knowledge Can Become Reusable Production IP

> **Hypothesis:** Industry knowledge can be represented as reusable domain models and production blueprints that accelerate creation and modernization of systems within specific industries.

### Summary

Industry domain models provide structured representations of business concepts and relationships that can be combined with technology blueprints to produce systems aligned with specific industry requirements.

### Implementation

Industry Knowledge → Domain Model → Blueprint + Configuration → Generated Industry System.

### Value — Today

Industry-specific production knowledge can provide a reusable foundation for new and modernized applications.

### Value — Tomorrow

Expanding industry coverage can allow the MSP to address a broader range of customer workloads with increasingly reusable production capability.

### Evidence

[Industry Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models/README.md); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md)

### Vision

Industry expertise becomes reusable production IP that can be applied repeatedly across customers and workloads.
